# Banana Bread

This is a pretty forgiving recipe for basic banana bread. You can add nuts or chocolate chips if you want. The key is not to overmix the batter. If you overmix, you'll get flat and rubbery banana bread. We don't use volume measurements for flour or sugar because they can take up different amounts of space under different conditions, so use a kitchen scale for those ingredients. We use an instant read thermometer inserted into the center of the loaf to know when it's done, because ovens and pans can vary.

## Equipment

* Kitchen scale
* Rubber spatula
* Wire whisk
* Standard loaf pan (~5" x 8")
* Parchment
* Oven
* Instant-read thermometer


## Ingredients

* 250 grams all-purpose flour
* 1 tsp baking soda
* 1 tsp ground cinnamon (can go higher if desired)
* 0.5 tsp salt
* 1 stick salted butter (115 grams)
* 190 grams light brown sugar
* 2 large eggs
* 80 grams (0.33 cups) sour cream
* 450-500 grams mashed overripe bananas (about 4-5 bananas, mash them with a potato masher or fork)
* 1 tsp vanilla extract (can go higher if desired)

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350°F
2. Whisk together flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon in a bowl
3. Lightly grease loaf pan and line it with parchment
4. Cream together butter and sugar until combined
5. Mix in eggs, one at a time
6. Mix in bananas, sour cream and vanilla
7. Gently fold in the dry ingredients with the spatula until just combined. **Do not overmix - overmixing is what makes rubbery and sad banana bread - stop mixing when there are no large dry pockets.**
8. Bake about 50-60 minutes. Take out when the temperature in the center of the loaf reaches 200°F
9. Cool at least 15 minutes before slicing
